# Apartment adviser: the owner's starting policies

These are personal valuation preferences and working assumptions supplied by the owner. They are not verified facts about a particular building, a market valuation formula or evidence that an individual seller is dishonest. Keep unknown details unknown. An explanation should identify which preference it used and which source observation supports its application.

## Personal valuation preferences

- Being closer to subway stations increases value to me.
- Being closer to a supermarket or hypermarket increases value to me; I prefer a hypermarket to a supermarket.
- Avoid ground-floor and top-floor apartments. I associate them with theft or building risks and expect a lower price.
- Included parking increases value to me because I expect to rely on a private car in Bucharest.
- Photographs showing no renovations should lower my valuation.
- Photographs showing old furniture should lower my valuation.

## Owner assumptions that need property-specific evidence

- I am concerned about buildings affected by the 1977 earthquake. I assume some subsequent repairs were poor; do not present this as a verified judgment about every building or infer structural safety from its age alone. My original description called the earthquake “7.5”; its magnitude and a building's damage/repair history have not been verified here.
- I expect older apartments may have poor electrical wiring and too few outlets for modern appliances. I expect upgrading them to be expensive; actual wiring condition and renovation costs remain unknown without evidence.
- My starting intuition was approximately two million cars for four million people in Bucharest. These figures are unverified context for my parking preference, not a model input or a checked demographic fact.
- I assume only about half of listings are in good faith. Treat this as my skepticism, not an established rate, a calibrated probability or a label that a particular seller is dishonest. Check claims against retained evidence and ask when important details are missing.
- Interpretations of renovations, furniture and condition from images remain inferences. A checked extraction does not turn hidden wiring, structural condition or repair quality into observed facts.

## Price comparison and teaching

Listing price is a source observation, separate from the personal price I teach. Never automatically train on listing prices as my preferred valuations. Large gaps in either direction mean I discard the listing; smaller gaps may inform considering or negotiating it. I have not set numeric thresholds or price adjustments yet.
